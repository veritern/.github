# Veritern

### Intelligent Change

Veritern works at the intersection of **people, organisations and technology**.

We build software, open-source tools and digital products that turn complex problems into practical systems: reducing friction, improving decisions and making the things people rely on work better.

Our approach to technology follows the same principles as our wider work: understand the real problem, challenge unnecessary complexity, make change practical, and build for the people who will actually live with the result.

## What you'll find here

This is the home of Veritern's public engineering and open-source work, including:

- software and digital products
- open-source tools and integrations
- supporting libraries and technical resources
- projects we believe are more useful when they can be inspected, used and improved by others

## How we build

We favour software that is:

- **Useful**: solving a real problem rather than adding technology for its own sake
- **Understandable**: clear enough to operate, maintain and improve
- **Responsible**: security, privacy and responsible technology are design concerns
- **Open where it helps**: sharing work when doing so can improve the wider ecosystem
- **Built for people**: technology should make work and everyday life better, not harder
